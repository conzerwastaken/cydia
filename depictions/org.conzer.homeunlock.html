HomeUnlock

iOS 10? In MY iOS 8? It's more likely than you think!

HomeUnlock redesigns the iOS 8 lock screen to use the iOS 10 "Press home to unlock" mechanism instead of sliding, and it keeps it in the iOS 8 style so it's not literally a rip of the iOS 10 lockscreen/

This tweak does NOT work on iOS 6, 7, or 9, and obviously not 10. I may port this to iOS 7/6/9 soon.
